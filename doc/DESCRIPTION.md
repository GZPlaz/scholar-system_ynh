Scholar System is a standards-based assessment platform for universities, in the spirit of Mastery Manager. Instructors build tests from a shared question bank aligned to learning outcomes, deliver them online or on paper, and get instant item analysis and standards-mastery reports.

### Features

- **Question bank** with six types: multiple choice, multiple select (optional partial credit), true/false, short answer, numeric (with tolerance) and essay. Questions are aligned to standards/learning outcomes, shareable between instructors, and importable from CSV.
- **Assessments**: availability windows, time limits, attempt limits, access codes for proctored rooms, per-student question and option shuffling, and a results-release policy.
- **Online testing**: autosave every few seconds, a server-enforced countdown timer, a question navigator with “flag for review”, and automatic submission when time runs out.
- **Paper testing**: printable test booklets and answer keys, and a full-page answer sheet for each student that you can **scan with a phone camera**: Scholar finds the sheet in the photo, knows whose it is from the printed code, reads the bubbles, and asks you only about unclear marks and handwritten answers. Keyed entry (`A`, `AC`, `T`…) and CSV import also work.
- **Grading**: automatic scoring, a grading queue for essays (grade by student or by question), score overrides with feedback, and automatic re-scoring when an answer key is corrected.
- **Reports**: score distribution, item analysis (difficulty, point-biserial, upper/lower discrimination, distractor analysis, Cronbach's α / KR-20), course gradebook, standards-mastery heat map, per-student reports and a student “My progress” page. Everything exports to CSV.
- **Administration**: roles (administrator, instructor, student), CSV import of users and rosters, configurable mastery thresholds and time zone, and an audit log.
- **RosarioSIS integration**: with the companion RosarioSIS plugin, course periods are created and kept in sync as Scholar courses, and test results are written into the RosarioSIS gradebook automatically.
- **YunoHost integration**: YunoHost users sign in with their usual account (LDAP) or straight through the YunoHost portal (SSO); students without a YunoHost account can get local accounts. Failed logins are protected by fail2ban.
