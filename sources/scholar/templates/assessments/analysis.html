Reading this report

A question with p below 0.30 is very hard; above 0.90, very easy. A point-biserial below 0.20 means the question barely separates strong from weak students, and a negative one usually points to a mis-keyed or ambiguous question: fix the key in the question editor and every attempt is re-scored. For classroom tests, reliability above 0.70 is generally acceptable.
